About our department

Our IT department plays an important role in ensuring that all computer systems, networks, and technology in the organization run smoothly. It provides technical support to staff, maintains computer hardware and software, protects data, and ensures the security of information systems. The department also helps solve technical problems and introduces new technologies to improve efficiency, communication, and productivity.
